prizes. Use deep uncertainty analysis, god-node and unexpected-edge discovery,
adversarial judges, What-If branching, and recursive estimator folding. Treat

## Research discipline

- Generate one causal mutation at a time.
- Predeclare its mathematical prediction, evidence unit, resource envelope,
  and kill conditions.
- Run the cheapest response-free falsifier first.
- Preserve passed components and identify the first broken causal link.
- Combine survivors only after ownership, residual covariance, and factorial
  interaction are defined.
- Keep immutable hashes, seeds, split provenance, reports, and manifests.
- Separate theorems, restricted lower bounds, empirical falsifiers,
  certificates, resource traces, component passes, and end-to-end efficacy.

## Honesty and legality boundary

- Obey the competition rules and installed cost model.
- Do not use private targets, hidden truth, public-row memorization, source-page
  artifacts, evaluator exploits, unbilled native arithmetic, API dependence,
  adaptive outcome selection, or disguised lookup tables.
- Do not infer a leaderboard rank or winning status from local component tests.
- Do not open a sealed efficacy or outcome cell before every prerequisite gate
  passes.
- Do not submit, upload, or replace the immutable formal champion without a
  separate explicit operational authorization.
