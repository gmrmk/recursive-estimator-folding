# Generation 6 deep what-if oracle

Status: **decision map, not a performance result**.

## Sharpened IF

Over the remaining Phase-1 window, what happens if the exact-control chain can
be made into one physical stream whose complete charged cost is at most `100B`
FLOPs, without assigning zero cost to the missing layer-bound Source211
producer, and whose sampled target-minus-control residual passes its frozen
variance gates?

The variable is the complete, inclusive composition. The magnitude is the gap
between the current guarded Kerdock estimator and an exact residual control
with a higher-cumulant oracle ceiling near `4.7e-8`. The horizon is the contest
deadline. Probabilities below are judgment weights over present evidence, not
measured frequencies.

pass to `3.55e-15`. M201 then killed generic repeated-label collapse, M203
killed standard terminal fusion, and M206 killed M204's arithmetic-identical

## Possibility space

### Omega -- the complete exact-control chain lands (2%)

M200 proves the one-pass ordering and provenance contract; a new M205 physical
K4/K31/K22 provider and complete caller replacement fit the strict envelope;
and the residual gate passes on generated whole-net holdouts before any contest
run. M204/M206 forbid treating the existing strict M151 emission as that call.

- Assumptions: the source coefficients are both cheaply observable and highly
  correlated with the target residual; the apparent background overlap is
  physical rather than workbook bookkeeping.
- Trigger: strict M200 parity and hostile tests pass, then the integrated trace
  is below budget with every allocation and terminal operation charged.
- Immediate consequence: open the frozen 24-cell M196 variance gate.
- Deadline response: run one fail-closed candidate beside, never instead of,
  the banked v3.1 artifact.
- Often missed: semantic composition alone earns no expected-score credit. The
  producer and residual variance are the information-bearing gates.

### Alpha -- semantics pass, economics remain blocked (42%)

The streaming fixture proves that M179, M198, and the M125b carrier can be
ordered without duplicate background construction, but no honest trace proves
that the physical source and terminal work fit the cap.

- Assumptions: the algebra is right; the existing protected worksheets cannot
  be reconciled into bit-identical native calls in time.
- Trigger: M200 passes while the inclusive trace remains `BLOCKED_OVERLAP`, or
  leaves less headroom than a real provider lower bound.
- Immediate consequence: preserve M200 as reusable semantic tissue and stop
  assigning it Phase-1 efficacy.
- Deadline response: ship the guarded Kerdock v3.1 candidate and the audited
  write-up; do not bolt an unpriced analytic branch onto it.
- Often missed: a blocked composition is progress because it isolates the
  first missing physical interface instead of relabeling it as mathematics.

### Delta -- two independent gates fail (25%)

The corrected H-source/H+1-weight stream fails strict parity or provenance,
and a conservative integrated trace is also above budget. The exact-control
branch cannot be repaired safely inside the Phase-1 window.

- Assumptions: delay-one indices or rank-face ownership do not commute with the
  desired streaming lifetime; duplicate state formation is unavoidable.
- Trigger: any frozen impulse/order test fails, source laundering succeeds, or
  complete charged cost exceeds the cap.
- Immediate consequence: kill M200 under its predeclared disposition without
  retuning; record the smallest counterexample.
- Deadline response: protect the six banked artifacts and avoid a budget-cliff
  submission.
- Often missed: failing both gates does not invalidate M178/M179/M198 locally;
  it invalidates this composition edge.

### Psi -- an untracked structural overlap appears (8%)

A one-process trace reveals that the exact background and source transport can
share an operand or contraction that the separate workbooks necessarily
double-counted, creating non-incremental headroom.

- Assumptions: the shared contraction has identical operands, dtype,
  accumulation order, lifetime, and output; it is not merely two similarly
  named Gaussian recurrences.
- Trigger: the event trace proves one producer with two consumers and no
  numerical-policy mismatch.
- Immediate consequence: freeze the exact overlap before measuring variance;
  independently reproduce the bill.
- Deadline response: spend the recovered budget only on the physical source,
  not on extra sampling that the mediant theorem says is dilutive.
- Often missed: overlap is an executable identity, not subtraction between two
  spreadsheet totals.

### Phi -- robustness, not lower public score, wins (12%)

The apparently superior public frontier depends on enforcement or evaluation
artifacts that fail private due diligence, while the guarded Kerdock estimator
survives every model and cost cliff.

- Assumptions: the organizer's private audit changes effective frontier order;
  the v3.1 healthy path remains reproducible.
- Trigger: official re-evaluation moves or invalidates artifact-sensitive
  entries; no honest analytic source clears the frozen gates.
- Immediate consequence: prioritize deterministic guards, margin, and a clear
  proof of rule compliance over speculative leaderboard mimicry.
- Deadline response: submit only verified tarballs.
- Often missed: this branch can win without improving the public point estimate,
  but it cannot be claimed before official results.

### Infinity -- the Phase-1 block becomes the Phase-2 engine (5%)

M200 and the source ABI do not change the Phase-1 score, but the exact
provenance, streaming, and rank-stratified primitives eliminate months of
ambiguity for the next shape or budget.

- Assumptions: Phase 2 preserves enough of the deep bias-free ReLU structure for
  the exact local identities to transfer.
- Trigger: local semantic gates pass while only the current cost envelope fails.
- Immediate consequence: retain the component as conditional tissue, with no
  retroactive Phase-1 promotion.
- Post-deadline response: rerun the cost oracle under the new specification
  before opening any efficacy gate.
- Often missed: the value is the dependency graph and negative knowledge, not
  the current score.

  `BLOCKED_OVERLAP`**.

- Only after all prior gates pass: compare whole-net held-out adjusted score
  against the guarded sampler.

### The one-percent insight

The scarce resource is not another fourth-order formula. It is a **lawfully
observable, layer-bound coefficient carried through one executable ownership
graph**. Almost every attractive failure supplied one of formula, coefficient,
carrier, or budget. None supplied all four at once. The winning mutation, if it
exists, is the first edge that makes those four identities coincide physically
rather than merely algebraically.
