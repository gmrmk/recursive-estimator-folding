# Deep What-If Oracle: crossing the exact-control throat

Status: response-free decision map. Subjective branch weights are planning
priors, not outcome probabilities. This document does not authorize an
efficacy read, champion change, submission, or winning claim.

## Frame

**Variable:** which single missing link can turn the exact-control architecture
into a deployable candidate.

**Magnitude:** cross the current throat without changing the frozen estimator
semantics: M163 must project below `100B`, M166 must compile below
`14.0191212B`, or the connected endpoint must certify every PSD stratum below
`606,720` operations per coefficient.

**Context:** 167 recorded mechanisms show an L-shaped frontier. Geometry is
near its sampling floor; exact high-order information is locally available but
fails at support, endpoint, product-rank, or dispatch walls.

## Possibility space

### Omega -- call fusion crosses the wall

Planning prior: 22%. Confidence: medium-low.

M169 preserves M163 exactly while batching the four independent post-`Z`
products. Billed arithmetic remains inside the compiler slot, packing is
preallocated, and five fresh hostile projections fall below `100B`.

Trigger: exact parity plus residual p99 below `7.149 ms` and zero failures.

Response: open the already-prespecified independent source-variance screen
once. Do not combine with M166 or tune packing after seeing efficacy.

What most analyses miss: M164 missed by wall time, not algebra or billed FLOPs;
one lawful call-graph change could reopen a branch that was correctly closed.

### Alpha -- batching is billed or packed back into failure

Planning prior: 38%. Confidence: medium.

FlopScope either lacks a true batch primitive, bills block cross-terms, or the
stack/copy/reshape traffic replaces dispatch savings. M169 remains above the
hostile cap despite fewer visible calls.

Trigger: any projection above `100B`, non-parity, extra reshape/copy bill, or
packing allocations in the hot path.

Response: preserve the API/call-overhead measurement and close this fusion.
Move effort to algebraic product elimination; do not retune batch sizes.

### Delta -- every exact route remains outside the joint envelope

Planning prior: 14%. Confidence: medium-low.

M169 fails wall, M170 proves at least six or seven product families are needed,
and M171 cannot certify a ten-node all-PSD provider. The validated sampler
remains champion and no new efficacy gate opens.

Trigger: all three prespecified premise kills.

Response: preserve the three no-go certificates and mutate the information
interface itself: a new exact identity, not another proposal or closure.

### Psi -- numerical exactness is recovered by a compensation channel

Planning prior: 8%. Confidence: low.

The M166 float32 control is made lawful by an error-free or rigorously bounded
shared compensation whose deterministic compiler and sampled residual account
for identical rounding. It stays below the slot and avoids seven float64
families.

Trigger: a source-level identity covers both coefficient rounding and GEMM
accumulation error with no hidden compute and less than `4.773B` added bill.

Response: create a separate mutation; do not smuggle this into M170 because it
changes dtype semantics. Require robust L2/Linf error envelopes before efficacy.

What most analyses miss: `c + (Delta-c)` is exact in real arithmetic but not
when the deterministic compiler rounds a global contraction differently from
the eventwise residual. Coefficient TwoSum alone is insufficient.

### Phi -- endpoint mathematics succeeds but the control is still useless

Planning prior: 12%. Confidence: low.

M171 certifies the generic coefficient within cost, but the complete
source-to-output residual does not contract. The analytic primitive is real;
the chosen deterministic control is not aligned with downstream influence.

Trigger: provider gates pass, then the predeclared source-variance upper-90 is
`>=.25`, p99 is `>1.25`, or width trend is adverse.

Response: preserve the provider as a major component. Change only the control
or conditioning information and rerun from the premise gate.

### Infinity -- ownership and compiler fusion interact superadditively

Planning prior: 6%. Confidence: low.

M167's physical owner retirement exposes products already computed by the
base fourth-cumulant path. A factorial compiler audit finds two M166 product
families can be reused rather than emitted, while M169 simultaneously removes
dispatches.

Trigger: a global common-subexpression graph proves exact reuse with no source
double count; the interaction saves more than the sum of isolated changes.

Response: only after individual premise passes, run all four compiler cells
`base`, `fusion`, `owner reuse`, and `both`; promote the composition only if its
interaction and hostile resource gates pass.

## Reverse oracle from the desired state

A credible winning candidate requires all of the following completed states:

1. exact or rigorously unbiased source ownership on every collision and PSD
   stratum;
2. a control whose support is contained in the physical target support;
3. connected-first rank-face cancellation before numerical differentiation;
4. one coalesced downstream carrier, not an all-output adjoint;
5. complete downstream residual variance upper-90 `<.25`, p99 `<=1.25`, and no
   adverse width trend;
6. five fresh hostile processes below the protected cap with zero failures;
7. a matched independent efficacy improvement whose paired uncertainty bound
   is below parity;
8. untouched final confirmation and immutable artifact hashes.

No current branch satisfies items 5--8. M169 attacks item 6, M170 attacks the
compiler part of item 6, and M171 attacks items 1 and 3.

## M177 update: algebra survives, installed numerical runtime does not

M177 defines and tests deterministic dispatch across SPD, exact rank-one,
zero-variance, and non-PSD strata. It fails the runtime/certificate gate:
FlopScope exposes billed univariate `Phi/phi` but no certified `Phi2` or
Owen-`T` evaluator, and no remainder contract from which to bound value and
Jacobian error. The known charged floor is `556` FLOPs per unordered positive
pair before that unavailable work. A generic zero-face JVP is also
path-underdetermined and must refuse rather than clip.

The next possible mutation is narrower than a full background recurrence:

```text
fixed/bounded-cost normalized Phi2/Owen-T evaluator
  + certified value/derivative remainder
  + explicit FlopScope scalar-work ledger
```

Until that unit passes, M176, M175, M172, variance, efficacy, and score remain
sealed. This is a runtime/certificate obstruction, not an information-
theoretic impossibility of bivariate Gaussian integration.
