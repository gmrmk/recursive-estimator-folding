# Adversarial judge: what the M155--M176 failure curve can actually support

Date: 2026-08-07  
Scope: frozen local fold ledger and frozen M155--M176 reports only.  No
response, truth, scorer, leaderboard, submission, or champion artifact was
read or changed.

## Verdict

The record supports a **qualitative dependency graph with a feedback cycle**,
not a measured or geometrically "nested L-shaped curve."  M175 and M176
invalidate the strongest monotone-migration reading.  The B=8 state ABI cannot
be instantiated from the current code because its exact background states and
Jacobians require an endpoint-complete, metered bivariate ReLU primitive.  The
purported final ABI bottleneck therefore loops back to the earlier
information/endpoint layer:

```text
collision/control geometry --> source-information error
          |                         |
          v                         v
exact endpoint kernels ------> labelled background/Jacobian state
          ^                         |
          |                         v
          +---- ABI audit <---- exact source conversion/carrier
                                    |
                                    v
                          dispatch/resource schedule
```

The apparent bends are real only in the limited causal sense that each new
audit exposes the next missing dependency in the attempted composition.  They
are not evidence that the global optimum, a universal lower bound, or a
winning inference must traverse these dependencies in one order.

There are 17 ledger leaves (M155--M176) and **17 distinct composite status
labels**.  Thus the labels are evidence-rich but not a common outcome scale:
there is no meaningful success percentage, slope, or curve fit.  Eight are
localized survivors, seven close a current implementation, certificate, or
integration link, and two establish restricted lower bounds.  **Zero of 17 is
an end-to-end, resource-inclusive, efficacy
validated estimator.**

## Is there a bottleneck migration?

Only the weaker claim of **successive dependency exposure** is supported:

1. M155/M156/M161 show that removing a masked-contraction cost can move the
   problem into residual tails; M163 and M166 then make collision support a
   geometric/algebraic design condition.
2. M165/M168/M171 show that once the connected object is formed correctly,
   endpoint information is constrained by rank stratum and near-parallel
   geometry, not merely by adding quadrature nodes.  M173 is evidence for one
   proposed repair, not for generic coverage.
3. M164/M169 show a mathematically identical compiler can flip from resource
   failure to conditional resource pass solely by call scheduling.
4. M174 shows that a local compiler pass cannot be composed into an inference
   path without provenance, ownership, and liveness of every state it assumes.
5. M175 shows that reducing the staging block to B=8 does not repair missing
   semantics.  M176 traces the first semantic break backward to the exact
   bivariate ReLU value-and-Jacobian primitive needed to create `V_l` and
   `(p,r,K,Hmu,Hv)` from the same preactivation state.

The stronger phrase "geometry -> information -> exact transport -> state ABI"
is rejected as a monotone law.  The defensible map is cyclic:
geometry determines endpoint regularity; endpoint kernels determine lawful
background/Jacobian state; that state determines whether source transport can
be staged; and staging/resource constraints determine which endpoint and
source representations are deployable.  M167/M172 owner semantics bridge
geometry and transport, while M161 was measured on a surrogate source proxy
rather than final inference error.

## Selection and non-comparability risks

- The branches are intentionally serial: descendants are designed around the
  predecessor's diagnosed failure.  That makes a diagonal-looking frontier
  expected even if another, untried family bypasses it.  It is not independent
  replication of a migration law.
- Gates differ radically: symbolic rank, static FLOPs, native p99, source
  variance proxy, high-precision anchor agreement, and ABI inspection cannot
  be rank-ordered as a single performance statistic.
- M161's six generated cells and M164/M169's five generated processes test
  different variables.  They cannot establish that batched M169 will improve
  M161's variance, nor that M172's source residual will survive transport.
- Survivor language is vulnerable to survivorship bias.  M156, M163, M165,
  M167, M168, M169, M172, and M173 each pass a localized gate while retaining
  at least one untested dependency.  Counting them as eight "wins" would be
  misleading.
- The hard results are scope-limited.  M155 is a bill for the installed
  generic realization; M170 is a lower bound only in its audited dense-product
  normal form.  Neither is an information-theoretic barrier to a legal
  estimator.
- M175/M176 were selected specifically to chase M174's first broken link.
  Their backward localization is strong evidence against a simple terminal
  ABI story, but not an estimate of how difficult a new bivariate primitive
  will be or whether another estimator family avoids it.

## Falsifiable consequences

1. **Primitive-first consequence.**  Before B=8 is tested, a single bundled
   FlopScope primitive must produce the bivariate ReLU value and the complete
   `(p,r,K,Hmu,Hv)` Jacobian blocks from the same `(a,C)` operation order,
   with a declared endpoint/fail-closed policy, no hidden clipping or variance
   floors, certified error accounting, and a generated target-shaped trace.
   Failure of any one condition keeps the M175 archive closed; passing them
   falsifies M176's *current-code* no-go without yet validating B=8.
2. **ABI-after-primitive consequence.**  Only after Consequence 1 passes may a
   labelled B=8 `[8,8,8,7]` archive be built.  It must preserve the pinned
   zero-order recurrence and direct diagonal Jacobian limits bit-for-bit or
   within a predeclared enclosure, and give an inclusive liveness trace.  If
   the primitive passes but the archive cannot preserve these identities,
   the break is genuinely ABI/ownership rather than endpoint mathematics.
3. **Scheduling consequence.**  If the ABI exists and its B=8 trace still
   violates the p99/cap gate, then call fusion alone is insufficient; the
   resource bottleneck is integrated state construction/liveness rather than
   merely M164's 155 compiler calls.  If it passes, M164's failure is correctly
   localized to scheduling, not exterior geometry.
4. **Source-conversion consequence.**  The `Source211 = (aaaa,aaab,aabb)` to
   `(b_l,B_l)` tangent formula must be derived and audited independently of the
   background primitive.  Shape compatibility is not evidence.  A failed
   ownership or dense-tensor parity test falsifies the M169/M172 carrier path
   even if the B=8 background archive passes.
5. **Owner-transfer consequence.**  Only after Consequences 1--4 pass may M172
   run its frozen source screen.  Its prediction is testable without outcome
   data: selective physical `[2,2]` transfer must meet the predeclared
   upper-90 residual/raw `< .25` and p99 `<=1.25`; otherwise `cE` is not a
   viable `[2,2]` control despite exact ownership algebra.
6. **Provider consequence.**  A general transverse rank-two provider must
   derive M173's connected amplitude/tangent envelopes for every dispatched
   component and retain its stated bounds over the declared chart.  One
   component that cannot certify them, or a positive-marginal nontransverse
   chart silently accepted, falsifies a claimed generalization.
7. **Escape-hatch consequence.**  Any claimed escape from M170 must exhibit
   an exact circuit outside its normal form, a full f64 scalar-work/copy/gather
   ledger, and native trace.  Merely batching or block-concatenating the two
   channels cannot reduce the proved family count.

## Adversarial disposition

Retain the dependency **cycle** as a planning aid, but do not describe it as a
quantified universal curve or monotone migration.  The next admissible causal
test is M176's bundled endpoint-complete/fail-closed, metered bivariate ReLU
value-and-Jacobian primitive.  B=8 staging, source conversion, M172 variance,
and efficacy remain downstream and sealed.  No claim about a winning entry
follows from the frozen M155--M176 record.
