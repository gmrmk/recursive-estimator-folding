## What this is

Every ledger record is coded on six categorical axes by regular expression over
its own strings. Nothing is inferred beyond what the strings say; an axis with no
evidence in the record is coded `unstated` (precision, convention) or `none`
(carrier, killtype). Mutual information then asks a single question per axis:
how many bits does knowing that axis tell you about whether the record lived or
died? Because MI computed on 276 records with many levels is biased upward, each
number is shown against a 200-shuffle permutation null of the same data.

Two targets are scored. `outcome_5class` is the full label (win / pass / kill /
protocol_kill / inconclusive), entropy 1.3720 bits over all 276 records.
`lived_vs_died` collapses that to a binary over the 257 records that actually got
a verdict, dropping the 19 inconclusive ones. That binary is the owner's
question -- the information between what did not work and what did -- and it is
the table to read first. The base rate of living is 0.2412 (62 of 257).

The attack lands. Delete the zonal_harmonic records and the carrier axis carries
nothing at all -- the whole signal is that one cell. So the correct statement is
not 'the carrier predicts survival'; it is 'candidates coded to a zonal/harmonic
carrier almost never survive, and no other axis value on any axis predicts
survival at this sample size'.

Second counter-hypothesis: zonal_harmonic could be a proxy for the analytic
source lineage rather than a carrier property. Survival by carrier within each

The zonal deficit reproduces inside more than one family, which argues against a
pure lineage artifact, but those cells hold 5 to 11 records each and cannot be
separated from mechanism at this sample size. Removing the transport family
leaves the carrier effect the same size and no longer significant, which is what
a real-but-underpowered effect looks like. Treat it as a hypothesis with a named
settling check, not a result: score one zonal/harmonic candidate on the promoted
Haar host and see whether it dies for a carrier reason or a cost reason.
