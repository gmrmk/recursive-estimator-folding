# WHestBench mathematical failure autopsy (M110--M176)

Frozen 2026-08-07.  This is an inference and mechanism postmortem, not an
efficacy claim, a leaderboard comparison, or an authorization to alter the
formal champion.  It uses the recursion rule that a killed item is a *specific
implementation at a stated gate*, not a dismissal of the surrounding idea.

## Executive diagnosis

The failed branches form a sharply informative curve.  The early branches
failed because they tried to infer the final directional residual from a
low-order, early-layer, or low-dimensional observable.  The middle branches
found that the missing information is carried by connected, repeated-label
non-Gaussian structure, but then encountered its two hard forms: a genuine
high-rank/state-completeness problem and collision-support concentration.
The later branches converted several of those obstructions into exact
identities, only to reveal the final bottleneck: endpoint-complete conditional
Gaussian primitives plus a lawful, metered state/ownership interface.

In short:

```text
low-degree directional information  --annihilated by design-->  no stable CV
four terminal moments               --not identifying-->        no terminal closure
local low-rank Jacobian             --tumbles across gates-->   no global subspace
collision source                    --dominates residual-->     no naive domain lift
exact control                       --needs dense products-->   rank/call wall
resource compiler                   --needs actual state ABI--> no deployable estimator yet
```

This is good news in the precise sense that the unknown is no longer an
undifferentiated "better math."  A viable analytic route must supply three
things simultaneously:

1. a **connected higher-order source** with correct collision ownership;
2. an **endpoint-stratified conditional-law provider** (not merely a smooth
   interior quadrature); and
3. a **labelled, FlopScope-metered background-to-tangent interface** whose
   liveness, numerical semantics, and cost are all actually realized.

No existing branch has all three.

**representation/conditioning failures**; the last two are **implementation
truth failures**.  Passing a later one never repairs an earlier one by itself.

## Autopsy by mechanism family

### A. Geometry saturated the easy spectrum

**Claimed mechanism.**  The Kerdock/MUB and antipodal framework in the dossier
uses exact spherical low-degree cancellation.  M110--M112 and M115 attempted
to add first-gate nodal occupancy, arcsine-pair phase, pair-Gram, and
projective arc-kernel controls on top of that carrier.

**First broken mathematical link.**  A quantity may be exactly centered,
gauge-correct, and statistically visible while still failing to predict the
directional residual left after a 5-design.  The design removes odd harmonics
and degrees through four.  The surviving error begins at even degree six and
is manufactured by downstream gate composition.  An early-axis amplitude or
pair field therefore lacks the output-specific downstream gate tensor needed
to give it a stable sign/phase.

**Evidence and scope.**

* The dossier's frame/5-design identities are algebraic: quadratic controls
  are annihilated on frames and degree-4 controls on the 5-design.  This is a
  mechanism constraint, not a fitted observation.
* M110's repaired spherical nodal probability and gauge law passed, yet all
  four held networks worsened (charged geometric ratio `2.1703`).  This is an
  empirical rejection of its independent-axis phase association, not of
  nodal geometry.
* M111's exact arcsine pair field also passed all invariance and numerical
  checks but worsened all four networks.  It rejects the *ungated transport*
  label `T`; it does not reject the arcsine field.
* M112's connected pair field `C` was exact-zero on independent frames but
  worsened all held networks.  Its forty-row kernel projection has no stable
  downstream phase; it is not a theorem that every gate kernel fails.
* M115's exact projective mean theorem survived, but its finite carrier
  worsened every network by 1.46--1.86 raw.  It rules out that first-layer
  observable/rank-128 construction, not all projective constructions.

**Preserved components.**  Exact spherical nodal probability, positive-gauge
repair, arcsine pair moments, centered pair field `C`, raw kernel audit, and
the all-network/independent-judge harness.

**Exact resurrection condition.**  Replace the missing label, not the ridge,
rank, seed, or fold: a new feature must provably capture a downstream
gate-conditioned phase and have an independently known/estimated mean whose
error is below the design's own standard error.  The dossier's
approximate-mean theorem makes this non-negotiable.  Merely adding biological,
transformer, or graph language without that observable is the same failed
mechanism in disguise.

### B. Four moments and generic Gaussian reclosure are not enough

**Claimed mechanism.**  Full-covariance Gaussian closure and descendants
(M113, M121--M131, M137) sought to inject finite-width `k3/k4` corrections
into a Gaussian tangent carrier, often through Hermite/Edgeworth vertices.

**First broken mathematical link.**  There are two separate failures that
must not be conflated:

1. repeated Gaussian reclosure loses the connected non-Gaussian source; and
2. even if terminal moments through four were given for free, they do not
   identify the terminal ReLU mean.

M137 proves (2): `N(0,1)` and a three-atom law share moments `(0,1,0,3)` but
have different `E[Z_+]`; under symmetry the compatible interval has width
`0.2113248654`.  This is a theorem-level information obstruction.  It is much
stronger than a bad Edgeworth fit.

**Evidence and scope.**

* The dossier's second-moment/oracle cap and the historical full-covariance
  result locate the bias in untracked higher cumulants.  These are model-class
  statements, not a lower bound for every weight-analytic method.
* M113's finite Hermite source representation failed its degree-16-to-18
  source drift gate by `74.35x`; that is a frozen representation falsifier.
* M120 kept the local Price/Jacobian algebra exactly but dropped the connected
  zero-diagonal `E`.  Complete-state error was `.0840` against `.05`, with all
  width/depth cells failing.  It rejects the omission under repeated nonnormal
  transport.  Random directional probes were misleadingly benign.
* M121/M122/M124/M125b separate cleanly: nonzero-mean bridge algebra and the
  first-Born forward tangent are preserved; the unresolved piece is the
  complete repeated-output `[2,1,1]` source plus a valid source factor.
* M128/M129 establish that a second-order response needs the Frechet tangent
  of the *state-dependent source*, not only its nominal value.  Mixed-float
  or partial source counts do not repair that missing derivative.

**Preserved components.**  M122's nonzero-mean Hermite/bridge inventory,
M125b's independently judged one-background forward tangent, M126's exact
easy repeated slices, M130's universal quadratic collision jet, and M137's
terminal interval/density-validity audits.

**Exact resurrection condition.**  Supply a correct conditional law or a
source carrying information beyond four terminal moments, then propagate both
value and Frechet response with explicit collision ownership.  A quartic
MaxEnt, two-Gaussian mixture, saddlepoint, or another finite-moment selector
is not a resurrection unless it has a separately proved law-specific
identification argument.

### C. The source is not merely large; collision topology is the god node

**Claimed mechanism.**  M117--M120 and M148--M151 tried to isolate or control
the `[2,1,1]`/`aaab`/`aabb` collision source through sparse actions, separated
vertices, Schur/Nystrom factors, copula controls, and direct adjoints.

**First broken mathematical link.**  Equality patterns are not a negligible
diagonal correction.  They are a different physical ownership class and,
after transport, can be the dominant residual energy.  Masking them breaks
the algebraic structure that enabled cheap contraction; retaining them
naively creates a Khatri/tensor action too expensive to run.

**Evidence and scope.**

* M117 exact counting gives `99,943,936` collision-support entries and a
  favorable subtotal `390.066B`; its `aabc` count is the cost god node.  This
  rules out that explicit sparse-action design before any numerical question.
* M118's rank-three quadratic jet is exact, yet three required banks plus
  transport and masking already cost `283.763B`.  It kills that separated
  representation, not the jet.
* M119's `K=(11^T+I)/4` counterexample shows why a low-rank PSD Schur factor
  cannot preserve the diagonal; a rank-eight truncation loses at least 48.4%
  on an admissible adjoint.  This is a restricted representation theorem.
* M148's literal copula/operator claim and M150's direct generic adjoint
  encounter the same rank/cost barrier.  M151's local B1 control survives as
  an identity but not as a generic compiled carrier.
* M155 makes the masked-star obstruction exact: a noncancelling star requires
  a `32896`-column symmetric Khatri action, `266.806B` across 31 layers.
  This is a theorem for that *masked dense-action form*, not a proof that all
  collision controls are impossible.

**Preserved components.**  Exact equality partition, Tallis/Price local
moment specification, the `aaab` quadratic jet, normal-ordered
diagonal/separable split, Khatri obstruction, and direct-adjoint local
identities.

**Exact resurrection condition.**  A new operator must preserve physical
orbit ownership while exposing an unmasked or genuinely structured action;
it cannot simply subtract collisions with an untracked proxy.  It must also
give a source Frechet derivative and a full-tail bound before it earns a
carrier test.

### D. Proposal, sampling, and learned-surrogate branches failed on tails or information

**Claimed mechanism.**  M131--M146, M152--M153 used hidden-edge/triple
sampling, graph-degree populations, local-factor conditioning, diagram
transformers, balanced incidence, positive trivariate envelopes, gate
split/merge, suffix-aware proposals, ACG transport, adaptive pilots, and a
weight student.

**First broken mathematical link.**  Exact marginal construction or a clever
proposal is not the objective; the relevant object is the *second moment of
the Hansen--Hurwitz-weighted residual under a causally frozen law*.  The
tail/variance and cost of the source dominate before the proposal's local
structure can help.  A learned map faces the same observability barrier as
the early controls: its input does not identify the per-network analytic
residual at the needed scale.

**Evidence and scope.**

* M131 preserves valid Gaussian-marginal construction and U-statistic logic,
  but neither exact boundary source nor sampled full source met the complete
  candidate gate.
* M133/M134/M138/M139/M140 establish useful exact proposal and sampling
  primitives but fail complete allocations, raw-degree or proposal-tail
  premises, or static cost/completeness.  These are not proofs that balanced
  sampling or factored conductance is useless in a different residual.
* M135 is important: conditional low rank is exactly valid for a *typed true*
  `diag(d)+UU^T` state, but generic covariance is not that state.  Replacing
  it by a low-rank approximation silently changes the law unless a density
  ratio/Price correction is carried.
* M136 and M152 reject their frozen learned causal heads/capacity/cost
  routes.  They do not prove a learned method is forbidden; they prove it
  needs a new observable and rigorous out-of-network generalization.
* M143--M146/153 show a different hard wall: pilot reuse/adaptation has to
  remain causally ordered and resource-safe.  M157/M160's self-hosted pilot
  retained correct ordering but exceeded hostile effective compute.

**Preserved components.**  Full-support HH discipline, independent-bank
U-statistic construction, exact typed-low-rank conditional law, frozen
proposal tangent convention, gauge-covariant fallback, pilot ordering audit,
and memoization guard.

**Exact resurrection condition.**  Predeclare a full-support proposal whose
importance-weight second moment and p99 are bounded on the same PSD/state
strata used by the source; couple it to a new sufficient observable rather
than learning from the old inadequate features.  Any adaptive proposal must
be frozen before the sampled residual and count every pilot operation.

### E. Local rank is real; global active-subspace inference is false

The dossiers' Jacobian observation is often misunderstood.  A pointwise
Jacobian can have rank about 15 because gates contract directions locally, yet
the leading subspaces at two inputs overlap only a little above chance.  Gate
patterns rotate the subspace.  The averaged sensitivity is near-isotropic;
on the 5-design's even-degree-at-least-six residual, capture by a fixed
`J`-subspace scales roughly as `(J/d)^6`.

This is the mathematical autopsy of static "slime-mold routing," mixture of
experts, cortical/graph attention, or transformer attenuation proposals:
they become a legitimate new branch only if they compute a **sample-local
route plus exact correction**.  A fixed global routing basis is simply an
active-subspace retry and inherits this failure.  It also explains why the
early first-layer controls and graph/weight students find correlations during
development yet fail at whole-network inference.

### F. Endpoint strata are a chart problem, not a quadrature-tuning problem

**Claimed mechanism.**  M147/M149/M154/M158/M159/M162/M165/M168/M171/M173
attempted an exact or certified noncentral bivariate/trivariate ReLU moment
and derivative provider for Gaussian source states.

**First broken mathematical link.**  The feasible PSD cone is stratified by
rank, marginal degeneracy, transversality, and moving ReLU kinks.  A fixed
interior quadrature is not uniformly accurate when a kink becomes a boundary
layer.  Further, a literal absolute binary64 coefficient tolerance is not
scale invariant.

**Evidence and scope.**

* M147's pair bridge is a passed component: endpoint limits and cancellation
  safe bivariate formulas exist, but generic nested endpoint coverage remains
  open.
* M158 proves the literal ABI impossible: an admissible rank-one coefficient
  can be farther than `2e-8` from its nearest float.  M159 repairs the *ABI*
  with dyadic scale metadata; it does not yet create the missing trivariate
  provider.
* M162's 87-node Plackett line misses the tolerance near correlation one, and
  the derivative grows to about `1.068e7` near the endpoint.  This kills that
  fixed rule, not Tallis reduction or all quadratures.
* M154 handles rank one exactly; M165 shows connected-first value-and-tangent
  subtraction regularizes the rank-one opening; M168 obtains the transverse
  positive-marginal rank-two anchor/tangent.  These are real algebraic
  survivors but do not cover nontransverse/zero-marginal/outward faces.
* M171 proves fixed GL10 has a rigorous remainder floor `.0020887` on a lawful
  `Phi(u/eta)` rank-two family, over `10^5` times tolerance.  That is a
  decisive rejection of a uniform fixed ten-node panel.
* M173 is the correct change of mechanism: split the step, scale the moving
  coordinate, and use a degree-nine boundary layer.  Its screened transverse
  chart bound is below tolerance, but the envelope, SPD, nontransverse,
  zero-face, and native-provider links remain open.  It is not yet generic
  credit.

**Preserved components.**  M147 pair primitive, M159 scale-normalized ABI,
M154 rank-one partition, M165 connected-first subtraction, M168 rank-two
wedge/coarea anchor, M173 parameter-scaled boundary layer.

**Exact resurrection condition.**  Build a finite chart atlas for all PSD
strata, with a symbolic cancellation/envelope on each chart and a proven
dispatch rule.  The generic primitive cannot be certified by simply raising
the number of fixed quadrature nodes.

### G. Collision controls: the decisive inversion was energy localization

**Claimed mechanism.**  M156 extended the masked target to a complete-domain
star control so it could be compiled in five dense products.  M161 then tested
its source variance.  M163/M166/M167/M172 tried to retain the cheap compiler
while correcting collision support and ownership.

**First broken mathematical link.**  M156's algebra and five-product compiler
are valid, but its control is nearly orthogonal to the physical collision
residual in the wrong way: collisions contain `99.9978--99.99999%` of residual
second moment and the pooled residual/raw ratio is `3.201e8`.  The original
hope -- that completing the domain would make masking cheap without changing
the variance geometry -- was false.

**Evidence and scope.**

* M161 is an empirical source-variance falsifier on six frozen generated
  backgrounds.  It kills that control law, not complete-domain compilation.
* M163's exterior determinant factor annihilates the dominant `iii`,
  `iik`, and `iji` collision patterns exactly.  The identity is good.  M164
  killed its particular deployment because 155 GEMM dispatches gave
  9.456--10.639ms residual wall, beyond the 7.149ms allowance.
* M166 nulls all collision patterns and passes parity/invariance, but its
  exact f64 form needs seven product families and costs `18.493B`; M170 proves
  the seven-family lower bound in its audited dense-product normal form.
  This does not eliminate a separately proved triangular/structured compiler.
* M167 fixes a key conceptual mistake: collisions are physical fourth-cumulant
  owners (`iii -> K4/6`, `iik/iji -> K31/3`, `ijj -> K22/2`).  M163's `ijj`
  is only a control, not physical `K22/2`.  M172's selective `[2,2]` fusion
  passes static ownership but has no opened development gate.

**Preserved components.**  M156 five-product complete-domain compiler,
M161 energy localization diagnostic, M163 exterior null identity, M166
orientation/collision-null identity, M167 exact owner map, and M172 selective
ownership ledger.

**Exact resurrection condition.**  Carry each physical collision owner and
the control separately, fuse only `K22/2-cE` where the identity permits, and
use a caller that actually owns the requisite labelled states.  The next
variance test must be fresh and predeclared; no raw efficacy conclusion may
be inferred from static ownership parity.

### H. Arithmetic wins are conditional until the real caller exists

**Claimed mechanism.**  M116b/c used exact L3 products; M169 fused M163's 155
GEMMs into two batch-axis calls.  M174--M176 audited whether those transforms
exist inside a lawful estimator.

**First broken mathematical link.**  Flop billing is not the whole resource
model.  Residual wall tracks dispatch count/allocation, and an optimized
transform is not deployable if it assumes an archive that no exact estimator
produces.

**Evidence and scope.**

* M116b/c preserve exact numerical L3 parity and low memory, but their one-shot
  traces show a near call-count law: 1024 calls -> `.6105s`; 512 -> `.3285s`,
  both over the `.170s` gate.  This closes that L3 family, not all arithmetic
  reductions.
* M169 is a genuine conditional resource survivor: bitwise parity, 155->2
  calls, `10.477B` bill, p99 `5.322ms`, and all hostile projection gates pass.
  Its condition is substantive: all 31 labelled `(W_l,V_l)` states must
  already be lawfully owned.
* M174 finds that condition false in current code: production is diagonal-only;
  fullcov is current-only, clipped/floored, and semantically different; there
  is no M163-slot-to-TangentState conversion.  M175's B=8 fallback has only
  conditional liveness/schedule evidence.  M176 locates the first broken
  primitive: no endpoint-complete, exact/certified, metered bivariate
  noncentral ReLU value-and-Jacobian provider.

**Preserved components.**  L3 numerical identity/call-law measurement; M169
two-axis batching compiler; M174 labelled archive specification; M175 B=8
bounded-liveness schedule; M176 exact recurrence contract.

**Exact resurrection condition.**  First implement and certify the bivariate
primitive; then build an exact metered `BackgroundArchive[l,W,mu,V,J,source]`;
then prove source-slot-to-tangent ownership; only after that run the B=8 or
two-axis integrated native trace.  Reusing a clipped/floored prototype is not
a conservative approximation here -- it changes the estimator's mathematical
state and invalidates the parity claim.

## Status discipline: theorem, restricted lower bound, empirical falsifier, chart failure

The ledger should be read with four different strengths:

* **Theorem-level constraints:** design annihilation identities; M137's
  four-moment non-identifiability; M158's literal floating-point ABI
  counterexample; M155's masked Khatri obstruction; M170's seven-family
  bound *within its audited dense-product normal form*; exact collision-owner
  identities.
* **Restricted lower bounds:** M117/M118 arithmetic counts and M119/M170 rank
  arguments only bind the stated source/action representations.  They invite a
  new representation, not a coefficient retune.
* **Empirical falsifiers:** M110--M112/M115 controls, M120 complete-state
  omission, M161 source variance, M164/M116 resource traces, and M157/M160
  hostile pilots.  Each is decisive for its frozen implementation and gate;
  none proves a universal impossibility theorem.
* **Contingent chart/interface failures:** M149/M162/M171 quadrature rules,
  M168 generic-provider credit, M173 incomplete chart coverage, and
  M174--M176 missing ABI.  These are the most productive unresolved leaves:
  their algebra often survives while coverage or integration does not.

This distinction prevents two symmetric errors: treating a proof of a narrow
representation obstruction as the death of all analytic inference, or treating
a beautiful local identity as permission to claim a generic estimator.

## Cross-failure deductions

1. **The relevant compression is topological, not spectral.**  Low-rank and
   low-degree compression both fail because they erase which indices coincide
   and which gate chart is active.  The reusable compression axis is instead
   orbit/owner topology: keep `K4`, `K31`, `K22` and their tangents labelled,
   then seek structured contractions inside each owner.

2. **Cancellation must happen before both transport and rounding.**  M120
   shows a locally small connected residual can become globally material under
   repeated transport.  M161 shows a control can be algebraically exact yet
   make collision variance enormous.  M162/M171 show a cancellation can be
   analytically real yet numerically invisible near a moving endpoint.  Thus a
   valid future control must expose its cancellation at the source-owner level,
   before dense transport, proposal weighting, and finite-precision endpoint
   evaluation.

3. **"More adaptation" is anti-evidence unless it introduces a new sufficient
   state.**  Fixed reweighting, static subspaces, trained phase heads, pilot
   proposals, and terminal moment selectors all adapt parameters while leaving
   the missing information unobserved.  The only promising adaptive form is a
   deterministic chart/ownership dispatch based on the exact current state;
   it changes representation, not a fitted coefficient.

4. **The resource path and the inference path have converged on the same
   missing primitive.**  The analytic branch needs an endpoint-complete
   noncentral conditional Gaussian law to create its source.  The compiler
   branch needs the same primitive to create an exact labelled background
   archive.  That makes the primitive a genuine shared bottleneck rather than
   plumbing.  Solving it produces both information and deployment leverage.

5. **The 5-design is not beaten by a better point set; it is bypassed by a
   better conditional expectation.**  The dossier's spectrum/ledger makes
   remaining sampling improvements small.  Any winner that materially crosses
   the indicated average-case sampling floor must reduce conditional variance
   with weight-derived information or compute higher-order structure, not
   merely mutate the frame geometry.

## Five highest-value preserved components

1. **M125b first-Born source-batched forward tangent:** an independently
   judged, inexpensive carrier once a valid source and background exist.
2. **M167/M172 collision-owner algebra:** exact `K4/K31/K22` mapping and the
   selective `[2,2]` residual ownership needed to prevent double counting.
3. **M154 + M165 + M168 + M173 face toolkit:** connected-first rank-one and
   transverse-rank-two anchors with a parameter-scaled boundary-layer repair.
4. **M169 two-axis call fusion:** a parity-checked 155-to-2 resource compiler,
   conditional on an honest state archive.
5. **M159 scale-normalized ABI plus M147 pair bridge:** the correct numerical
   contract and endpoint-safe bivariate foundation for the generic provider.

## Three nonobvious combined insights

1. **A universal source representation should be indexed by `(owner, PSD
chart, tangent)` rather than by tensor rank.**  Owner labels solve collision
   correctness; charts solve endpoint singularities; tangents solve transport.
   A rank-only factorization is exactly what repeatedly lost one of these.

2. **The correct “attention” is a fail-closed mathematical router.**  It
   should dispatch rank-one/rank-two/interior/zero-face formulas using exact
   state predicates, return a certified remainder, and preserve a full-support
   fallback.  That is the rigorous version of the biological/mixture-of-experts
   intuition; learned soft attention is neither required nor presently
   justified.

3. **Call fusion is valuable only after semantic fusion.**  M169 proved the
   runtime benefit; M174--M176 proved that batching unowned states is a
   mirage.  The next architecture must fuse the recurrence, archive, source
   ownership, and carrier contract first; batching comes last as a compiler
   lowering step.

## Immediate implication for the next mutation

Do not reopen a control, a fixed quadrature panel, a rank cap, or a learned
phase head by parameter drift.  The narrow honest route is:

```text
endpoint-complete bivariate value/J primitive
  -> labelled exact background archive (B=8 liveness discipline)
  -> explicit Source211/owner-to-TangentState map
  -> charted rank-face provider with certified remainders
  -> fresh source-variance gate for K22/2-cE
  -> only then integrated fused-resource trace and efficacy ladder
```

Each arrow is a separate falsifiable mechanism.  Until the first three exist,
the formal sampling champion remains the only deployable champion.
