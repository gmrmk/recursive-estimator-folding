## Objective and boundary

Win honestly on a fresh private rerun, not merely on the public score oracle.
The validated L1 archive and sealed M71 archive are immutable controls.  No
candidate may replace them because it sounds novel, passes an in-sample fit, or
matches public leaderboard telemetry.  Promotion requires a predeclared
mechanism, exact symmetry/mean law where claimed, full cost accounting, fresh
generated-network evidence, and an independent result judge.

The recursion rule is:

`failure -> localized missing edge -> one mechanism-changing child -> theorem audit -> one-shot falsifier -> fold`

No seed, ridge, feature, split, threshold, or normalization is retuned after a
candidate sees its binding outcomes.
