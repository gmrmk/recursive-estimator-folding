## Why this is the only high-upside class left

The observed failure curve is L-shaped:

```text
point placement / sampling
    -> variance falls to the 5-design high-degree floor
cheap moment closure
    -> cost passes, fixed-network higher-cumulant bias remains
exact higher-order algebra
    -> information passes locally, tensor rank / endpoints / wall cost fail
learned routing or biological attenuation
    -> can choose work, but cannot invent an unknown exact mean
```

The champion's design is already within roughly 5--11 percent of the matched
linear-sampling floor. More sampling geometry can therefore deliver only a
small constant factor at prohibitive point count. Exact final-layer third and
fourth cumulants, by contrast, have an oracle mean-MSE ceiling around
`4.7e-8`, roughly five times below the present raw design error. That is the
only measured basin large enough to plausibly change the prize outcome.

The architecture uses an analytic approximation only as a **control**. M148's
identity samples the full target-minus-control residual, so an imperfect
control changes variance rather than bias. This is the central repair to every
moment-closure failure.

not the true full-rank Source211 law.

M204 constructs a two-active-node Rademacher state from `diag(V)` and obtains

```text
u_i = sqrt(V_ii)/sqrt(n_active)
d_i = V_ii-u_i^2
dtilde_ijk = -2 u_i^2 u_j u_k
```

strict headroom before conversion, terminal work, copies, or wall.

### B. New terminal-contraction circuit -- standard class closed

M203 gives the strongest explicit standard repair: stack the three `aaab`
channels into one inner-dimension `3n` rectangle and the two `aabb` channels
into one `2n` rectangle, then apply recursive Winograd. Depth five is optimal
in that family, but terminal plus ideal triangular projection still costs
`10.543779520B`, above even isolated M151 headroom before omitted work.

Allowed inspirations include polarization, generating functions, Fourier
phase coding, tensor-network elimination, and reversible accumulation. The
gate is exact arithmetic rank plus FlopScope bill, not aesthetic compression.

Generic channel minors retain ranks three and two, so polarization, phases,
and concatenation are basis changes. Reopen only with an explicit nonstandard
simultaneous bilinear decomposition, a generic-cell proof, and a complete f64
operation/copy bill below strict headroom. This is no longer a prime candidate.

M202 supplies the exact minimum counterexample. For
`f_epsilon=[x1]_+-(1-epsilon)[x2]_+`, signed/absolute mass is

signed coarea identity only. Reopen this class solely with an analytically
normalized owned-output-facet generator plus separate ESS and cost proofs.
