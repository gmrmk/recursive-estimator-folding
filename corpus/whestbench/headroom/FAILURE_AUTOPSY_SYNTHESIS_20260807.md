## What the false positives were actually telling us

1. **A local pass is a coordinate, not a candidate.** M169 passes scheduling;
   M172 passes ownership; M173 passes one boundary layer. Their conjunction is
   undefined until the shared states and semantics exist.
2. **Algebraic unbiasedness and operational exactness are different.** An
   identity can conserve expectation yet explode variance (M156/M161), or be
   exact in real arithmetic but unaffordable/uncertified under the installed
   runtime (M166/M177).
3. **The downstream influence norm is the truth metric.** Coefficient error,
   Frobenius rank, local covariance capture, and source amplitude are proxies.
   They repeatedly reverse after nonnormal gate transport.
4. **The endpoint is part of the model, not a numerical corner case.** Legal
   PSD states approach rank faces and zero marginals. Clipping, ridges, and one
   fixed panel silently change the estimator precisely where derivatives are
   largest.
5. **Instrumentation belongs inside the theorem.** A product identity without
   calls, copies, liveness, dtype, and provider cost is not a contest-valid
   complexity result.

## The new representation coordinate

Repeated rank-only and feature-only compression failures point to a different
state key:

```text
SourceAtom(owner, psd_chart, feasible_tangent, coefficient, remainder)
```

- `owner` is the physical equality orbit (`[4]`, `[3,1]`, `[2,2]`, or
  distinct-label source), preventing double count and zero-target tails.
- `psd_chart` is interior, rank-one, transverse rank-two, nontransverse,
  zero-marginal, or refusal, preventing hidden endpoint extrapolation.
- `feasible_tangent` records the one-sided cone direction and stops
  path-underdetermined Jacobians from masquerading as derivatives.
- `coefficient` and `remainder` travel together; a value without a certified
  numerical/transport error is not a lawful atom.

This is topological compression rather than spectral compression. It does not
claim that the number of atoms is yet affordable; it supplies the coordinate
system in which affordability can be tested without losing semantics.

## Rigorous translation of the biological/router intuition

The useful mixture-of-experts analogue is not a learned soft attenuator. It is
a deterministic fail-closed mathematical router:

```text
exact state predicates
  -> select owner + PSD chart + feasible tangent formula
  -> emit certified atom or refuse to the full-support fallback
  -> aggregate once through the coalesced forward tangent
```

This retains the good part of biological routing—local specialization and
resource allocation—without inventing a weight-trained selector whose
generalization is unproved. The router changes representation because the
state has changed chart; it does not adapt after seeing an error.

## Incorporated process laws

1. **First-break rule:** mutate the earliest missing dependency only. A
   downstream optimization cannot repair an absent upstream object.
2. **Owner-before-control rule:** cancellation is attached to physical source
   ownership before importance weighting, transport, or rounding.
3. **Chart-before-quadrature rule:** subtract rank-face anchors and scale the
   singular coordinate before choosing any numerical panel.
4. **Semantic-before-call fusion rule:** fuse recurrence, labels, ownership,
   and carrier meaning before batching arrays.
5. **Full-norm-before-rank rule:** no compression gets credit from selected
   directions when the state is repeatedly transported.
6. **Inclusive-cost rule:** every call, allocation, materialization, cast,
   gather, and provider operation is part of the mechanism.
7. **No resurrection by parameter drift:** a killed span reopens only when a
   new causal edge, circuit class, chart, or information variable is supplied.
