# Fourier-enhanced neural systems and exact-mean WHest distillation

Date: 2026-08-06

Primary source: Enze Xu and Minghan Chen, **Fourier-enhanced Neural
Networks For Systems Biology Applications**, arXiv:2502.07129 (2025).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07129

## What the source actually does

SB-FNN is a physics-informed surrogate for ODE/PDE systems biology models. It
uses embedded Fourier neural layers, an adaptive mixture of activation
functions, and a cyclic/variance penalty designed for oscillatory biological
dynamics. The reported experiments use 12 Fourier modes, four Fourier layers,
adaptive candidates `{ReLU,tanh,sin,GELU,ELU,Softplus}`, Latin-hypercube points,
and physics residual/initial-condition losses on six models (repressilator,
SIR variants, and one-/two-dimensional Turing systems). It is an arXiv
preprint, not a theorem about arbitrary ReLU networks.

Its direct assumptions do not match WHest:

- SB-FNN exploits ordered time/space grids and oscillatory dynamics.
- WHest input coordinates are exchangeable under orthogonal/permutation
  symmetries. Applying a one-dimensional FFT across neuron index would impose a
  false adjacency and violate the estimator symmetry.
- Training a large Fourier neural operator inside `predict()` is incompatible
  with the cost budget.
- The paper's cyclic variance penalty has no justified counterpart unless a
  depth-frequency premise first finds an oscillatory mode.

## Transferable mechanism 1: depth-spectrum diagnostics

Layer number is genuinely ordered. For any invariant scalar or two-lane
response sequence `r_l`, a DCT over `l=1..32` is an admissible cheap diagnostic.
It can test whether the sequence is smooth, oscillatory, or endpoint-dominated
without assigning geometry to neuron labels. A fixed low-mode DCT summary may
enter the Młynarski optimization utility or flatworm ladder, but only after a
fresh cleanroom premise shows stable spectral concentration. Cost is `O(L log
L)` per invariant channel.

## Transferable mechanism 2: an exact-mean Fourier control student

The stronger adaptation is per-network teacher--student multifidelity. Let the
deep disclosed network be `f` and choose a small feature student

```text
g(x) = A h(x).
```

Fit only `A` on pilot teacher evaluations and estimate the coupled residual on
an independent or cross-fitted set:

```text
I_hat = E_exact[g(X)] + mean_residual(f(X)-g(X)).
```

Conditional on the pilot, this is unbiased whenever `E_exact[g]` is exact and
the residual sample is independent. The student is useful only if its variance
reduction repays pilot, fitting, feature, and lost-teacher-evaluation costs.

### Gaussian Fourier features

For `X ~ N(0,I)` and a fixed weight/Jacobian-derived frequency `omega`,

```text
E cos(omega^T X + b) = exp(-||omega||^2/2) cos(b),
E sin(omega^T X + b) = exp(-||omega||^2/2) sin(b).
```

Thus paired sine/cosine features give a non-polynomial student with closed-form
mean. Frequencies must be generated by a frozen target-free rule from the
weights or deterministic isotropic randomization; selecting them from observed
reference error is forbidden.

### Spherical Gegenbauer features

After exact radialization, let `u` be uniform on `S^255`. For any unit
direction `v` and normalized zonal harmonic `P_l`,

```text
E_u P_l(v.u) = 0,  l > 0.
```

The current antipodal 5-design already annihilates odd degrees and degrees two
and four. Therefore a meaningful descendant starts at the design-surviving
even degrees, especially `l=6,8,...`; a low-degree Fourier/polynomial student
is subsumed by the earlier failed control-variate experiments. Directions can
come from first-layer gate normals or frozen pilot Jacobian/adjoint directions,
but pointwise subspace tumbling requires a bank rather than one global vector.

The fitted control is

```text
g_j(u) = sum_(k,l) A[j,k,l] P_l(v_k.u),
E_u[g_j] = 0.
```

This directly targets the post-design high-degree directional residual. Feature
evaluation uses a stable Gegenbauer recurrence and has no network-forward cost;
the coefficient fit and `H^T F` multiplication must still be billed.

## Predeclared factorial

On fresh cleanroom networks compare at matched total cost:

1. no student;
2. shallow ReLU ridge student with analytic Gaussian mean;
3. Gaussian sine/cosine student;
4. spherical degree-`>=6` Gegenbauer student.

Freeze feature count, degrees/frequency scales, weight/Jacobian direction rule,
pilot/residual split, regularization, and random seeds before teacher outcomes.
Cross-fit if pilot samples are reused. Report:

- variance of `f-g` relative to `f` per output and network;
- adjusted variance after the exact teacher/pilot/fit cost ledger;
- degree-band capture and correlation with the known design residual;
- coefficient conditioning and sensitivity;
- permutation, input-rotation distributional, antipodal, and seed tests;
- worst-case FlopScope shapes and residual wall-time estimate.

Promotion requires a predeclared material reduction on whole fresh networks,
not an in-sample fit. If only degrees `<=5` are captured, or the effective
variance-per-cost is no better than the parent, kill that specified student and
preserve the exact-mean feature identities for a different direction source.
