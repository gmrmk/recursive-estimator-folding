   parameters conceal 2,880 raw-arm weights with zero input and gradient,
   leaving an effective upper bound of 2,441 live parameters versus 5,321.
   Any future binding full-versus-control comparison must demonstrate live
   inputs and gradients, not merely equal tensors or counts.

   post-training shuffle is descriptive only. The active-null repair preserves

   capacity does not repair M105's nested exception overwrite. A single writer

   otherwise a failed split/index/seed/shape cannot be audited.

   frozen arrays and honestly billed permitted runtime computation. The
