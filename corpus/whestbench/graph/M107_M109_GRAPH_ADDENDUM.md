## Evidence chains and actionable inference

### M107: proof survives; covariance transfer does not

For `U` uniform on `S^255`, every nonconstant normalized zonal
`P_l(a.U)` has zero spherical mean. Weights-only uniform and squared-path
mixtures preserve this conditional law. Fitting output slopes on frames 0-15
and applying raw controls only on independent frames 16-31 protects the mean,
but does not guarantee variance reduction.

The held adjusted/base trace ratios are
`[1.2349683,1.2533812,1.3637836,1.1312104]`; geometric and pooled information
ratios are `1.2431025` and `1.2561862`, exact bootstrap q05/q90 is
`[1.1819513,1.3025892]`, and the charged proxy is `2.5181387`. Training-only
ratios `0.68854--0.76158` reverse above parity on every held network. The graph
therefore localizes failure at stable frame-level covariance/coefficient
transfer, while preserving radialization, W1 orientation, first-pre reuse,
the degree recurrence, exact-zero law, cross-fit boundary, and evidence
harness.

### M108: cymatic amplitude is a tail trap

The fixed heat band and `B^2-1` both have legitimate harmonic equations and
mean laws. Exact no-network calculations nevertheless give
`E[B^4]=2.046953098540e23`, `Var(B^2-1)=2.046953098540e23`, and standardized
linear-band kurtosis `3.123400620022e18` even after a complete frame and 256
axes. This creates the explicit chain

```text
scaled harmonic amplitude -> needle-like fourth moment -> preexecution kill
```

The failure is not repaired by rescaling, squaring, aggregation, or swapping
the almost-collinear heat and Poisson labels.

### M109: boundedness repairs one premise, but the source is invalid

The draft attempted to replace only the failed tail edge with

```text
1(|a.U| <= 1/16) - p_draft.
```

The indicator is bounded, so it removes M108's amplitude blow-up. The static
judge nevertheless finds that the draft integrates density exponent 126 while
`S^255` requires 126.5. The correct probability is
`0.6817415182413444070...`, not `0.6821303412444035966...`; the implemented
atom therefore has mean about `-3.8882300305919e-4`, invalidating its claimed
exact-zero bias class.

The squared-path mixture independently violates the positive ReLU gauge: a
function-preserving hidden rescaling multiplies its path component by
`c_j^-2` while leaving the normalized axis unchanged. Uniform mixing does not
have this defect. The current M109 source is therefore killed before freeze or
network execution. A corrected uniform bounded-occupancy family is a distinct,
unimplemented salvage branch requiring fresh centering, invariance, float32
decode, frame-moment, cost, and held-covariance gates. M107's training-to-held
reversal remains its principal efficacy falsifier; there is no basis for a
threshold sweep or champion mutation.
