## 3. What does not revive

Withdrawing the R0 replacement does **not** restore the original claim that
residual harmonic energy is equipartitioned per mode. The pre-charter record
never established that statement. In particular:

- the degree-`l` design-error operator and the residual-field harmonic energy
  coefficients are different mathematical objects;
- flatness of the degree-4 design operator does not imply equipartition of the
  residual over harmonic degrees or modes;
- a chi-squared one-point amplitude marginal does not determine a harmonic
  spectrum;
- family-local output observations cannot be silently promoted into a global
  spectral-tail theorem.

The corrected status is therefore **OPEN**, not a reversion to the older
mechanism.
