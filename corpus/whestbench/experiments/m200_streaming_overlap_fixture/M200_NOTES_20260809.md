## Adversarial gates

The unit suite passes 11/11 response-free checks, including a frozen-artifact
hash/scope audit:

- full-archive and labelled-carrier helpers monkeypatched to fail during the
  stream, proving the stream does not call them;
- float32 input is rejected rather than silently cast;
- cross-layer/reordered, duplicate, and terminal-reinjected packets fail;
- equal-valued copied `W`, `Vprev`, `a`, `C`, and `mu` fail object/token binding;
- `C_k` and `V_k` are rejected as substitutes for upstream `V_(k-1)`;
- a source issued under the same M179 context but alternate `W/Vprev` cannot
  be wrapper-relabelled into the current layer;
- post-issuance weight mutation fails closed; and
- every M125b transport records the identity of the exact current M179
  Jacobian and `W_k`; previous-state logical buffers remain live through their
  successor layer before release.

## What passed, and what remains blocked

M200 proves that the already-existing generated M179, M198, and M125b pieces
can be joined without retaining a full background archive, a dense retained
`distinct_211`, or one suffix state per source. Each event records operation,
float64 dtype, shape, logical buffer ID, digest, birth/death order,
alias/copy class, and an explicit native-cost disposition. See
`M200_EVENT_LIVENESS_LEDGER_20260809.json`.

The fixture source is deliberately nonzero but its provider cost is explicitly
unknown. M198 conversion cost and all target-native costs are likewise
unmeasured. The layer and source receipts are cooperative in-process integrity
checks, not cryptographic signatures or a hostile-code authority boundary.
Those limitations are why this remains a component pass rather than a cost,
variance, or estimator promotion.
