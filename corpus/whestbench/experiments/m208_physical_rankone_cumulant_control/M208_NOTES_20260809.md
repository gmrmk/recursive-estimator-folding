# M208 result -- p-only compiler killed by the omitted `[1,1,1,1]` wedge

M208 failed its first predeclared parity gate. The physical rank-one owner
table over `[4]`, `[3,1]`, `[2,2]`, and `[2,1,1]` is not the complete
rank-one fourth tensor once width is at least four. The proposed p-only source
also contains the all-four-distinct hidden-label partition, which the current
Source211 owner does not own.

The failure is exact and localized. At width three the gate passes because no
four distinct labels exist. At widths four through seven the maximum source
errors are respectively `0.4639776`, `9.7439064`, `7.0577790`, and
`226.3405962`, all far above `5e-10`. An independent enumeration of the
`[1,1,1,1]` wedge repairs p-only parity to at most `3.08e-13`.

Therefore this is not a numerical or implementation defect:

```text
p-only full rank-one tensor
  = physical [4]+[3,1]+[2,2]+[2,1,1] source
  + physical [1,1,1,1] wedge.
```

The current exact-control architecture has no owned, certified, affordable
physical `[1,1,1,1]` target/residual provider. Adding the p-only compiler to
M205 would silently add that wedge and bias the current owner decomposition.
M208 is consequently `KILLED_MISSING_ALL_FOUR_DISTINCT_OWNER_SECTOR`.

Preserve the p-only full-tensor identity and the explicit wedge oracle. A
lawful descendant must either own and subtract the wedge, prove it cancels
against an already-executed `B_other` call, or choose a structurally supported
control for which the wedge is identically zero. It may not relabel the wedge
as Source211 or infer cost/variance credit from width three.
