# M209 closeout -- exact arithmetic survivor, frozen wall kill

M209 discovered and verified a genuine resource edge: M205's matrix is a Gram
matrix, so only one triangular half must be contracted. A depth-3 block tree,
batched across all 31 layers, reproduces the complete dense M205 source while
using 15 matmul calls and an exact matmul bill of `1,167,925,248`. The complete
metered compiler is `1,226,651,648`, leaving `760,219,824` of M199's strict
no-replacement component slot. Ten response-free tests pass, including dense
source parity, exact integer algebra, gauge/permutation/zero-state behavior,
and hostile layer/dtype/epoch binding.

That does **not** promote the frozen M209 schedule. Once the missing binding
contract was added, five clean target-shape processes gave residual wall times
of 1.2168--1.5387 ms. Under the predeclared hostile five-times projection,
three processes fit but two exceed the strict `1,986,871,472` slot; the worst
projection is `1,996,001,554.48`, an overage of `9,130,082.48`. The stop rule
therefore kills M209 without changing its depth or rerunning a tuned variant.

The earlier `M209_NATIVE_TRACE_*` files are retained as pre-binding
implementation diagnostics. They are not the gate. The authoritative files
are `M209_BOUND_NATIVE_TRACE_*`, produced after reorder, duplicate, epoch, and
float32 attacks were made fail-closed.

## Preserved tissue

- `B=U^T U` admits an exact triangular recursive compiler; M206's dense
  `2.084B` floor is not a general lower bound on this new arithmetic class.
- All 31 layer products can be lawful batch axes after their factors exist.
- The inclusive arithmetic result fits by `760.22M`, peak RSS is only
  `167.35 MiB`, persistent arrays are `93.36 MiB`, and no rank-3 coefficient
  table is retained.
- The remaining failure is dispatch/Python residual, not algebra, memory,
  numerical error, or billed FLOPs.
