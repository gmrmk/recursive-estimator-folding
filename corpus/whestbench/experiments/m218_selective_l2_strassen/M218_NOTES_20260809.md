## Mechanism actually tested

Only

```text
A=(S^2)^T S and E=(S^3)^T S
```

changed. M215's collision equations, strict-distinct ownership, legacy
physical collision routing, receipt/binding, and
`D=(S^2)^T(S^2)` symmetric recursion are unchanged.

For each product, classic Strassen is applied exactly twice. Rather than 49
matmul calls, M218 vectorizes all leaf products into one shape
`(7,7,31,64,64)` matmul. The right leaf pack for `S` is built once and reused
for both products. A single 7-branch level buffer is successively reused for
right packing, left packing, and recombination.
