## Collision-partition proof

Let `H(i,j,k)` be M205's half-owned source feature and let
`c(i,j,k)=-2u_i^2u_ju_k`. The collision domain is the disjoint union

```text
i=j=k,  i=j!=k,  i=k!=j,  j=k!=i.
```

Set `S=diag(u)W`, `p=S^T1`, `B=S^TS`, `rho=diag(B)`, and

```text
A=(S^2)^TS,  t=(S^3)^T1,  E=(S^3)^TS,  D=(S^2)^T(S^2).
```

For `aaab`, the four disjoint pieces are

```text
i=j!=k : -12 [diag(p)A-E]
i=k!=j :  -6 [diag(p)A+t p^T-2E]
j=k!=i : -12 [diag(rho)B-E]
i=j=k  : -12 E.
```

Their sum is

```text
Ccol_aaab = -18 diag(p)A -6t p^T -12diag(rho)B +24E.
```

For `aabb`, the two `[3,1]` orientations together contribute

```text
-12[A diag(p)+diag(p)A^T-2D],
```

the strict `[2,2]` piece contributes

```text
-4rho rho^T -8(B hadamard B)+12D,
```

and the all-equal row contributes `-12D`. Therefore

```text
Ccol_aabb = -12[A diag(p)+diag(p)A^T]
             -4rho rho^T -8(B hadamard B)+24D.
```

`Ccol_aaaa=diag(Ccol_aaab)`. Thus `Cfull-Ccol` contains precisely and only
pairwise-distinct ordered rows. The `D` term is mandatory; dropping it repeats
M208's missing-all-four-equal-owner failure in another guise.
