be replaced by any M161 diagnostic cell, and no result can authorize a
competition or final-output claim.

## Preconditions

Do **not** execute this protocol unless all are independently recorded:

1. The M163 five-product source compiler has exhaustive small-width parity,
   collision-null, permutation, positive-gauge, isotropic-zero fallback, and
   full-domain conservation checks passing.
2. A native target-shaped FlopScope trace, including correlation
   normalization, exterior arithmetic, copies, allocation high-water, and
   residual wall time, fits the inclusive `14.019121200B` compiler slot. The
   current `13.076947200B` static worksheet is not a native trace.
3. The exact M147-compatible distinct `[2,1,1]` provider accepts every cell
   with its frozen certificate. A refusal is a provider block, not permission
   to approximate, clip, ridge, remove a cell, or change its order.
4. Complete-domain ownership is rechecked: physical target is zero on every
   collision, the M163 control is zero on `iii`, `iik`, and `iji`, and only
   `ijj` may have a sampled residual. No other collision owner is inserted.

## Frozen q0, source proxy, and gates

Use M161's unchanged target-width collision mass `eta=0.011688232421875` and
uniform distributions within distinct/collision strata. It is fixed before
target construction, with no pilot, adaptation, retuning, or row deletion.

For every cell enumerate the exact M147 distinct target and the full flattened
M156 source-slot vector `(aaaa, aaab, aabb)`. The primary estimate is the
finite-population fixed-q0 HH trace variance of the raw target source versus
the M163 residual source. The scalar coefficient proxy is diagnostic only.

Development passes only if the one-sided paired bootstrap upper-90 across all
six cells is `< .25`, the maximum raw-normalized source p99 residual
contribution is `<=1.25`, the residual `ijj` p99 is `<=1.25` of raw p99, and
the mean source-ratio slope versus width is nonpositive. Record `iii`, `iik`,
`iji`, and `ijj` second-moment contributions separately, even though the first
three should be bitwise zero under M163.

If development passes, execute the confirmation cells once with unchanged
code, q0, provider, and gates. Any failure kills this configuration. The
result remains a pre-transport source premise: it cannot claim final-output
efficacy without a separately audited one-carrier integration.
